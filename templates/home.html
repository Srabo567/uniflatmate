How it works

Four steps, no money involved

1

Verify

Sign up with your university email. The domain has to match the campus you picked, so everyone here is a real student.

2

Answer four questions

Smoking, sleep schedule, study environment, monthly budget. That is the whole profile.

3

Get scored

Every other student on your campus is scored against you, with the reasoning shown next to the number.

4

Send a request

The other student accepts or declines. Rent is arranged face to face — this site never touches it.
